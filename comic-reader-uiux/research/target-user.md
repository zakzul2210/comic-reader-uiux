# Target User

## Primary Users

Teenagers and young adults who frequently read comics online.

## User Characteristics

- Frequently use smartphones and computers.
- Read comics online regularly.
- Prefer simple and fast navigation.
- Search for comics based on title or genre.
- Prefer a comfortable reading interface.

## User Needs

1. Easy comic discovery.
2. Fast search.
3. Clear comic information.
4. Simple chapter navigation.
5. Comfortable reading experience.
